Our Story

PrintBro was born out of a simple frustration — students in Bangladesh were paying too much for printing and wasting hours standing in queues, especially during exam season.

What started as a small dorm-room idea grew into Bangladesh's most trusted student printing service. Today, we proudly serve students from 50+ universities with one promise: fast, affordable, and reliable printing — every single time.

We understand student life — last-minute assignments, thesis deadlines, and tight budgets. That's exactly why PrintBro exists: to save your time, money, and stress so you can focus on what truly matters — your success.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?
Simply use our Order page — select print options, fill in your details, and submit. Then email your file to support.printbro@gmail.com and confirm on WhatsApp. Done!
What file formats do you accept?
We accept PDF, DOCX, DOC, PPT, PPTX, JPG, PNG, and most common formats. For the best printing results, we recommend sending files as PDF.
How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery takes 2–4 hours within Dhaka. Outside Dhaka may take 24–48 hours. Emergency same-day delivery is available on request via WhatsApp.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept bKash, Nagad, Rocket, bank transfer, and cash on delivery. Payment details are shared on WhatsApp after order confirmation.
Is my document safe with you?
100%! Your documents are fully confidential and automatically deleted after printing. Your privacy is our top priority.
Do you offer bulk discounts?
Yes! Orders of 100+ pages automatically get discounted rates. For very large orders (500+ pages), contact us on WhatsApp for custom pricing.
